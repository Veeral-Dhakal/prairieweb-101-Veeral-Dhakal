About

Explore Alberta Trails is your ultimate guide to discovering the finest outdoor adventures across the province. From the majestic peaks of the Rocky Mountains to tranquil foothill paths, our curated routes set a new standard for outdoor exploration.

Featured Trails

  • Johnston Canyon:
    Location: Banff National Park
    Distance: 5.1 km return
    Difficulty: Easy / Moderate
    Highlights: Waterfalls, deep canyon gorges, catwalks
  • Ha Ling Peak:
    Canmore, AB - 7.2 km (Steep summit climb)
  • Crypt Lake Trail:
    Waterton Lakes National Park - 17.2 km (Includes tunnel and ladder sections)
  • Plain of Six Glaciers:
    Lake Louise - 14.6 km with panoramic glacier views
